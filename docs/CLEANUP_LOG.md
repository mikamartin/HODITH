## feat/insights-trends-tag-timing

**Scope:** PROGRESS.md's Story C T7 — a tenth Trends detector, `TAG_TIMING`: whether a tag's own events cluster into one weekday or time-of-day bucket beyond the Case's overall rhythm there.

**Feasibility ruling (this item's own design decision, resolved in plan mode before implementation):** the spec's ask (`computeRhythmStats`-shaped per-tag counts against the Case's overall rhythm) is a categorical over-concentration question, not a difference-in-means one like every prior `Pattern` detector — `labelShufflePValue`'s two-list-of-doubles shape doesn't fit. Settled on a max-positive-bucket-deviation statistic (largest tagged-share-over-baseline-share gain across a dimension's buckets), tested via the shared `permutationPValue` primitive directly with a custom shuffle closure (trend slope's "no third bespoke test" precedent), re-taking the same max on every shuffle so the null distribution accounts for the observed statistic being a maximum over several candidate buckets (change point's own look-elsewhere fix, applied to a new statistic). First plan draft recommended shipping only the four-bucket time-of-day dimension and dropping the seven-bucket weekday dimension outright (the roster's usual 15-tagged-event floor divides too thin across 7 buckets, ~2 events/bucket, for a peak to mean anything) — the user explicitly asked to keep weekday clustering rather than drop it, so the design was reworked to gate each dimension with its own sample-size floor (28 tagged / 80 Case-wide for weekday vs. 15/45 for time-of-day) instead. The descriptive floor also needed its own precedent: reused tag share shift's dual absolute-and-relative floor shape (15pp absolute AND 50% relative) rather than a single flat bar, since a flat bar would be easier to clear on weekday's lower per-bucket baseline share (~14%) than time-of-day's (~25%).
