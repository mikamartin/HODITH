Share cards — template prototype

Same theme tokens (colors/shapes) as the Case Detail and Triggers prototypes, applied to spec §13's story/square share card. Fonts approximated, not embedded. All copy here is illustrative, reacting material for the visual direction — not final Voice-layer strings (those get written in Kotlin once this pass is signed off).

*Duration/Intensity only offered for Cases that track them — same conditional as the in-app Insights tab.

What changed from the last pass

Rhythm's cells were invisible — a real bug: adding the day/time legend meant vertically centering label text next to the grid, which required align-items: center on the grid container. Grid items default to stretch, which is what made the empty .cell divs visible as squares before; switching to center let them collapse to zero height since they have no content of their own. Fixed by giving cells an explicit size instead of relying on stretch.

Reality section brought back — whenever Hunch vs. Reality isn't shown (always on Square, and on Story when there's no Hunch or it's toggled off), a compact Reality section now shows total events and observation length ("14 events" / "60 days observed") — the two numbers the old Evidence beat carried, restored as a fallback rather than as its own permanent beat. This also resolves the "empty card" gap flagged last pass (no-Hunch Story / Square with nothing else selected): there's always at least this.

Unchanged from last pass: Rhythm's day-of-week/time-of-day legend, Frequency's "by week" unit subtitle, Insights sections reusing the real InsightsCard chrome and per-section layouts, and content-driven card length (min-height only, no clipping).
